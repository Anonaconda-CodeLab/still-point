Before you start

Please read this once.

{{APP_NAME}} is a wellness tool for recovery tracking and relaxed breathing. It is not a medical device.

  • It is for general wellness, training and relaxation only.
  • Do not use it to diagnose, treat, rule out or monitor any illness or heart condition, or to make decisions about medicines or treatment.
  • Slow breathing can make some people light-headed. If that happens, stop and breathe normally. If you are pregnant or have a heart, lung or blood-pressure condition, check with your doctor before slow-breathing practice.
  • Do the standing test only where you can sit down straight away, and stop if you feel faint.
  • If you feel unwell, with chest pain or pressure, fainting, severe breathlessness, or a racing or very irregular heartbeat, call your local emergency number now.

Open session

No guide, no counting. Sit or lie comfortably, breathe however feels natural, and let your mind settle. With the strap on, your heart rate and HRV are tracked quietly and summarised at the end.

Resonance pace

Your heart rate rises as you breathe in and falls as you breathe out. At one particular pace, usually between 4.5 and 6.5 breaths a minute, that swing is largest. This test guides you through five paces for two minutes each and finds yours.
